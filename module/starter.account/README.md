# starter.account

A personal account area on top of [starter.cms](../starter.cms/README.md).

- *My account* with the login form, and below it *Profile* (email address and password), *Passkeys*,
  *Two-factor* (authenticator app and backup codes) and *Devices*.
- The superuser pages for passkeys, authenticator apps and backup codes.
- Switches on the *User account* section of the privacy policy.
